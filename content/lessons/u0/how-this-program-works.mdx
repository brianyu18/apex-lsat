## The loop

Everything here feeds one cycle:

<Steps>
1. **Learn** — a short lesson with an interactive diagram, then a five-question check. Pass 4 of 5 to mark it done.
2. **Drill** — one question at a time, untimed, around your level. The engine serves questions you can *just about* get, and stretches above that a set share of the time.
3. **Review** — every miss (and every lucky guess) lands in your inbox. You tag *why* it happened and write one takeaway.
4. **Re-drill** — tagged misses come back in 3, 5, then 10 days, paired with a fresh question of the same shape, until you've beaten the pattern three times.
5. **Test** — timed sections and full tests with built-in blind review, once the method is reliable.
</Steps>

## Confidence on every answer

Before you check an answer you'll pick **sure**, **leaning**, or **guess**. This isn't busywork:

- Answers under 10 seconds, or marked *guess*, don't count toward your skill rating. Lucky guesses would otherwise inflate it and push you into questions you aren't ready for.
- Over time the app compares confidence to results. If you're "sure" and right only 80% of the time, that's a specific, fixable problem — overconfidence on the last two choices — and no amount of extra volume fixes it.

<KeyIdea>
The drill isn't scored on how many you get right. It's scored on whether your *sure* answers are right and whether your misses stop repeating.
</KeyIdea>

## Why "why you missed" matters more than "what you missed"

Every prep program can tell you your Flaw accuracy is 71%. That number doesn't tell you what to do. A miss has a cause, and the causes have different cures:

| Cause | What it looks like | Cure |
|---|---|---|
| Misread | fast, confident, wrong; picked the reversed answer | slow the first read; re-read the stem |
| Logic gap | didn't see the structure; fell for a force or scope trap | back to the lesson; untimed drill on the pattern |
| Trap | saw the gap, got pulled by a tempting choice | name the killing words; prephrase before reading choices |
| Time pressure | guessed or bailed | pacing checkpoints and skip rules, not more content |
| Careless | switched off the right answer | trust the first read unless you can *prove* the switch |

The review screen pre-suggests a cause from your timing, confidence and the trap pattern of the answer you picked. You confirm or correct it in one tap. Those tags drive what you drill next.

## Skill ratings, plainly

Each question type and each cross-type pattern has a rating. Difficulty levels 1–4 map to opponent strength. Answer a level-3 question right and your rating rises; miss a level-1 and it falls. A rating around 75 on the displayed 0–100 scale means you're reliable through level 3; 90 means you're close to acing the section. You'll see these on the dashboard, and the drill picks levels from them unless you override.

## What the check below does

Five questions, two of the most common types. This one *is* graded: 4 of 5 completes the lesson. Misses go to your inbox — go tag them when you're done, so you see the whole loop once before the real work starts.
